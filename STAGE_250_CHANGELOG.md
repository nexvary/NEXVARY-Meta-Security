## Stage 250 feature set

- 100 simulated labs across 10 tracks.
- OWASP/CWE metadata and per-track research checklists.
- Vulnerable vs Patched simulated request/response comparison.
- Favorites, notes, scores, attempts, XP, rank, streak and achievements.
- Four guided learning paths.
- Ten-question exam with one question from every track.
- Exam history and weak-area recommendations.
- Local activity log.
- Arabic / English UI with RTL / LTR switching.
- Text scaling, high contrast and reduced-motion settings.
- Local text-report export through Android Storage Access Framework.
- Native Android share sheet for reports.
- No INTERNET permission.
- WebView external-request interception plus CSP connect-src none.
- Android 15 / API 35.

## Safety model

This app is intentionally a local training simulator. It does not probe, scan or exploit Meta, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, or any external target. Historical cases are explicitly separated from synthetic training cases.
